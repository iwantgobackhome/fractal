New telescope discovers a nearby world

By Ada Example

A new telescope has found a nearby world with unusual clouds. Scientists described the observations in a paper published this week, noting that the atmosphere differs from earlier discoveries.

How it was found

The team compared repeated observations over several nights. The measurements suggest a dense upper atmosphere and give researchers a new target for future study.

We need more observations to understand this world.

Follow-up instruments are expected to examine the planet later this year. The researchers cautioned that the current result does not establish whether the world could support life.
