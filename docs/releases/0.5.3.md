# News Papers 0.5.3

News Papers 0.5.3 makes the Android reader faster and keeps phones paired across desktop restarts.

- **Smoother Android reading.** Pinch zoom now scales the page view while your fingers move and lays the pages out once when you let go, keeping the point under your fingers in place. Page sizes are read once when a PDF opens, so scrolling never waits for a page render, and high-resolution detail is drawn only after zooming or scrolling stops.
- **Local-first Android annotations.** Ink, highlights and notes are saved on the phone without contacting the Hub on every stroke. The phone syncs when you open or leave a paper, when the app goes to the background, from Tools › Sync now, and every 15 minutes in the background. Changes from the desktop appear the next time you open the paper or sync.
- **Stable Hub address.** The desktop Hub keeps the same port across restarts (7327 by default), so a paired phone keeps working after the desktop app or the PC restarts. When the phone cannot reach the Hub, it explains what to check and how to pair again by QR code. After this update the desktop app moves to its stable port once, so pair phones again one time.
- **Android details.** Settings shows the installed version, and the library header uses the News Papers mark.
