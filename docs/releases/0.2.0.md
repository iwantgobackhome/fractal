# Fractal 0.2.0

Fractal 0.2.0 is [available now](https://github.com/iwantgobackhome/fractal/releases/tag/v0.2.0), published on October 1, 2026. It brings its scholarly reading and discovery workspace to the desktop and native Android companion, with a persistent research library and more direct publication reading.

- Scholarly desktop and Android layouts, with Papers, News and Topics discovery and native article source/translation split reading.
- Saved and Recent views, nested folders, tags and library search distinguish bookmarks from reading activity.
- Durable question and explanation history retains context and status; highlights, annotations, pen ink and movable sticky notes stay with each paper.
- Exact character selection in the original PDF and translated text, with original, translation and side-by-side reading modes.
- Available publication PDFs open inside Fractal from the primary Read action. Cached PDFs retain their immutable identity; inaccessible, unsupported or failed acquisition shows an explicit fallback to the publisher rather than claiming a PDF is available.
- Discovery first tries suitable captioned figures from paper HTML and suitable article content images for news. Lazy images, responsive variants and later suitable candidates are supported; source thumbnails are fallbacks. Logos and placeholders are excluded, and verified raster bytes are cached locally. Missing or failed images leave text and reading actions available. Local PDF figure extraction is not part of this thumbnail path, and every paper is not guaranteed an image or an accessible PDF.
- Existing Codex/Claude accounts, translation language storage, library exports, pairing and local data migration remain part of the workspace.
