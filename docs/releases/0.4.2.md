# News Papers 0.4.2

News Papers 0.4.2 fixes moving collapsed answer cards.

- **Desktop.** A collapsed answer card ("?" or "i") moved only when grabbed by its thin edge, because a press on the marker itself was ignored. The whole marker now drags it; a click without moving still expands it.
- **Android.** A collapsed answer marker could only be tapped. It can now be dragged, and its new position is saved and synced.
- The source passage, figure or equation stays highlighted while a collapsed marker is dragged.
