# News Papers 0.5.1

News Papers 0.5.1 lets books over 300 pages be translated whole again.

- **Whole-book translation.** Books over 300 pages show **Translate whole book** next to **Translate pages**. Pages are sent one at a time; if the AI subscription limit is reached the job pauses and can be resumed later.
- **Widening sections.** After translating page ranges, **Translate whole book** translates only the remaining pages and keeps the sections already done.
