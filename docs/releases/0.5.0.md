# News Papers 0.5.0

News Papers 0.5.0 fixes mobile reading, ink and sync, sends figures and regions to the AI as images, and opens long books.

- **Zoom and pan (Android).** Pages no longer go blank while zooming or panning: the last rendering stays until a sharper one is ready, and zoomed pages render only the visible tile at full resolution. The ink surface is sized to the visible viewport instead of the zoomed page, which fixes crashes after repeated zooming.
- **Realtime sync.** The Hub streams change events (`/api/sync/events`). Android pushes changes within about 0.3 s and pulls on each event; the desktop refreshes on events and keeps the 10 s poll only as a fallback.
- **Pen.** Strokes are saved in one batch off the main thread, and stylus input is unbuffered, so the pen no longer lags. Shapes (line, arrow, rectangle, ellipse) preview while drawing and render as shapes on the desktop.
- **Lasso.** Long-press inside a lasso selection with a finger, then drag, to move every selected stroke together; one undo restores them.
- **Figures and equations.** Explain buttons are hidden until a figure or equation is tapped.
- **Text selection (Android).** Dragging across a paragraph selects whole lines instead of only the left part.
- **Translation view (Android).** Figures and equations use one consistent scale.
- **Image questions.** Figure, equation, table and region questions send the cropped image to Claude or Codex, with the question shown as a short attachment chip instead of long pasted text. Desktop and Android.
- **Long PDFs.** The limit is now 3000 pages and 300 MiB. Extraction runs in a worker thread. Documents over 300 pages are translated in 30-page sections chosen in the reader.
- **Pairing.** The connect screen explains that the phone and computer must share a Wi-Fi network or both run Tailscale, and offers to open Tailscale.
