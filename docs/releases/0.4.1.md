# News Papers 0.4.1

News Papers 0.4.1 keeps answers on the page and adds license notices to the apps.

## Answer cards

- **Pinned to the page.** An answer card stays where you put it on the paper and scrolls with the page instead of following the screen.
- **Kept until deleted.** Asking something new opens another card and leaves earlier ones in place. Cards are saved with their position and reappear when you reopen the paper, so you can read an answer again without asking again. Positions sync between the desktop and Android.
- **Collapse and delete.** Collapsing leaves a small marker, "?" for a question or "i" for an explanation, at the card's position. Deleting removes the card from the page. The conversation stays in History, and opening it there brings the card back.
- **Source highlight.** While you hover over or drag a card, the passage it is about is highlighted: the selected text for a question, or the figure, table or equation for an explanation.

## License notices

- The desktop and Android apps now include the News Papers license (Apache-2.0), the MIT notice of [PaperRead](https://github.com/nkjunbc/PaperRead), which News Papers started from, and the licenses of the libraries they ship with. The desktop app also includes the Electron and Chromium notices.
- Open them from **Settings → Open-source licenses**.
